About

Hello — I'm a senior at the University of Washington studying computer science and physics, advised by Andrea Coladangelo. I'm a member of the CS theory and quantum computation research groups. My research is on quantum Gibbs state preparation and the mixing of Lindbladian dynamics, though I'm broadly interested in quantum algorithms and complexity.

This summer I'm a research intern at IBM Quantum at the T.J. Watson Research Center. I also write problems for Science Olympiad.

Research

How long does an open quantum system take to thermalize, and what about a Hamiltonian forces it to happen quickly? I'm working toward a proof that exponential decay of correlations implies polynomial mixing time for the Chen–Kastoryano–Gilyén Gibbs sampler on commuting, geometrically local Hamiltonians at fixed inverse temperature.

The technical core is relating static correlation structure — clustering of correlations, decay of conditional mutual information — to dynamic quantities like the spectral gap of the Lindbladian, using quasi-factorization of relative entropy together with locality of the Gibbs state.
